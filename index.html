What booking a session involves

Three steps, about a minute. Nothing is charged on this site — you pay your tutor directly at the session, at their hourly rate.

  1. 1

    Pick a tutor

    Filter by subject, your child's grade, and hourly rate. Each profile lists what the tutor covers, how they run a session, and who they work best with.

  2. 2

    Choose an open hour

    You see that tutor's real openings for the next three weeks. Taken hours are shown as booked so you are never picking a time that is already gone.

  3. 3

    Tell us about your student

    Your name and email, your child's first name and grade, and the subject they need. Dana confirms by email, usually the same evening.

Questions parents ask us

How do we pay?

You pay your tutor directly at the end of the session, at the hourly rate on their profile. There is nothing to pay on this website and no card is collected.

Where do sessions happen?

Most sessions are at the public library on Main Street or online over video, whichever you prefer. Dana confirms the location in the email that follows your booking.

What if my child needs a subject that is not listed?

Ask us. We are a small service and Dana often knows a tutor who is not on the site yet. Email hello@abctutoring.example.

Can we book the same tutor every week?

Yes. Book the first session here, then tell your tutor you would like a standing hour and Dana will hold it for you. There is no package or contract to sign.
